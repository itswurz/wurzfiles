Overview

The Wurz Files is a database of people from a Discord server. This site contains information about their activities, behaviors, and other stuff that happened on the server.

Each person has a file with their info. Everything here is supposed to be secret and classified and all that.

Classification Levels

  • ALPHA - Important people
  • BETA - Regular members
  • GAMMA - Sometimes there
  • OMEGA - Not active anymore
  • SIGMA - Being investigated

Subject Files

Click on the subjects section to see files on different people from the server. Each file has information about what they do, how they act, and stuff they were involved in.

Incidents

This section has records of notable things that happened on the server. Drama, arguments, funny moments, whatever seemed important enough to document.

Archive

Old records and files from people who left or things that happened a while ago. Everything gets archived here so there's a complete record.
